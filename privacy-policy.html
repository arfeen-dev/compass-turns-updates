Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026 · Developer: Avens Interactive

This Privacy Policy explains how Compass Turns - Aviation (the “App”) handles information when you use the Android application. For privacy questions, contact arfeenziavoudine@gmail.com.

At a glance

The App is an educational compass-turn calculator and simulation tool. It does not require an account. Its calculation and simulation features operate locally. The App can access the internet to check for updates and direct you to an app distribution service.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may save your selected training region, custom compass correction values, appearance preference, and update-reminder choices using local device storage. These settings are used to remember your choices and operate the App. They are not intentionally uploaded by the App to our servers.

2. Update checks and external services

For update checking, the App may contact a public release manifest hosted through GitHub Pages. A normal internet request may expose technical connection information, such as an IP address, request time, and browser or app network metadata, to the hosting provider. GitHub processes such information under its own applicable privacy terms.

When installed through Google Play, the App may use Google Play services for app-update availability and installation. Google may process device, account, and diagnostic information under Google's own privacy policies. Opening a GitHub download page or Google Play may transfer you to a third-party service.

3. Personal information and accounts

The current App does not include user registration, sign-in, a social profile, or an in-app form requesting your name, email address, or payment details. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request.

4. Advertising and payments

The version described by this policy does not integrate an advertising SDK or offer in-app payments. If advertising, analytics, account creation, or payments are introduced, this policy and the applicable Google Play Data safety disclosures will be reviewed and updated before those features are released.

5. Sharing of information

We do not intentionally sell personal information collected through the App. We do not operate an App backend that receives compass headings, correction profiles, or saved preferences. Third-party hosting or distribution services may process technical information when you use their services, as described above.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally saved App preferences remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it, subject to device backup and restore behavior. You may contact us to request deletion of information that you have provided directly by email, subject to legal or security retention needs. We cannot delete information independently held by Google, GitHub, or your device provider; consult their privacy controls.

7. Children's privacy

The App is designed for aviation students and other learners, and is not specifically directed to children. It does not provide social networking or account registration. If we learn that we have received personal information from a child through direct communication, we will address the request in accordance with applicable law.

8. Security

The App uses local storage for preferences and relies on the security of the device and the relevant external services for their respective operations. No method of electronic storage or network communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the App changes. The revised version will be published at this page with an updated effective date. Significant changes may also be communicated through release notes or in-app notices where appropriate.
